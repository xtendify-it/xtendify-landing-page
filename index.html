Untouchable Monoliths

The core systems (ERP, CRM) of large organizations contain years' worth of custom logic embedded directly in the source code ("In-App Extensibility"). This approach makes any software update a lengthy, risky, and incredibly expensive process.

The "Clean Core" Revolution

Separate the transactional stability of the central information system from the pace of cutting-edge innovation. XTENDIFY operates purely in a side-by-side manner, meaning the company's legacy ERP or CRM systems remain standard and unaltered.
